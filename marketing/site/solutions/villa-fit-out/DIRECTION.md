## Direction contract

THESIS: a fit-out company sees its own job as a schedule of trades with money pinned to it: stages by days, a payment at the start of
each, the bill of quantities under the stage that executes it, and who executes it (own team, own factory, supplier).
OWN-WORLD: white ground, ink type, one teal (amber only for «supplier» and «draft» status chips, as in the app), IBM Plex Sans
Arabic, hairlines, #F5F7F9 stages; one CC0 gypsum-ceiling photograph; product fragments with fit-out data.
