# Surface brief: site-next home (direction D)

Scope: marketing/site-next home, first viewport plus the «من أول اتصال إلى آخر توقيع» journey. Mode: Persuade. Action: request a demo.
Audience: owners and managers of retailers and showrooms selling ready and custom products, some with a factory or field team.
Proof: the real OpsOne screens of one demo order ORD-2026-00178 whose numbers add up. No customers, numbers, prices or testimonials.
Constraints: approved Arabic copy verbatim; no sectors; «مصنع» never «ورشة»; never call the system «عربي»; two-part demo names, one-letter avatars, no women's images; RTL first.

## Direction contract

FORM: user-steered fusion of direction C (colour identity) and direction A (motion idea); A's seed seed:432f6bedd5f7e99f, shapes re-derived after the A/C critique.
THESIS: one order travels one straight line through the business, and every place it reaches lights up and opens its real OpsOne screen. Refuses the category's dashboard-screenshot hero and A's overlapping cutaway.
OWN-WORLD: white ground on every band (hairlines separate them), ink #0B1B33 type, a single teal #0E7C72 used for the travelled line, the reached and live stations, progress, and the headline's second line «في مكان واحد» (kept from direction C, whose colour identity the owner asked to keep; finish review asked for ink, declined on that ground). Neutral #DDE3EA hairlines. Stations are 48px circles with one Lucide icon and one label. Product screens are white cards with a hairline ring and offset soft drop; chips in the app's own state colours.
STORY: the headline promises one place; the line under it proves it in five stations (المعرض، العقد والدفعة، المصنع، التسليم، موقع العميل); the journey section uses the same grammar (numbered dots on one line that fills to the active one) for the nine approved steps; the steps are not mapped to the five stations because several (customer files, changing how the system works) belong to no single place; the page closes on the demo action.
FIRST VIEWPORT: headline at the start, lede and two actions at the end, then the five-station line and the live station's screen opening directly beneath it, all inside 900px at 1440. On phones the line is vertical and the selected station's screen opens inline under its row; the live screen's header sits inside 812px.
SIGNATURE: the line draws from the first station to the live one (1200ms strong ease-in-out), stations light as it reaches them, the order tag rides the head of the line, and clicking any station slides its screen under it (320ms) with a 220ms content swap. The journey's line fills with scroll and the sticky screen swaps per step. Reduced motion shows the end state and keeps only short opacity swaps.
